NOWAIT y lock_timeout: no esperar para siempre
==============================================

Demostración 4b de 08_transacciones.sql. Mientras A tiene la fila bloqueada, B prueba tres cosas:
NOWAIT (error inmediato), lock_timeout de 3 s (error a los 3 s) y un SELECT normal (lee sin esperar).

Tiempo   Sesión  Sentencia / resultado
------------------------------------------------------------------------
  0.08 s  [A] SQL> BEGIN;
  0.08 s  [A] SQL> SELECT id_lote, id_sede, stock FROM clinica.existencia WHERE id_lote = 10 AND id_sede = 1 FOR UPDATE;
  0.08 s  [A]       id_lote | id_sede | stock 
  0.08 s  [A]      ---------+---------+-------
  0.08 s  [A]            10 |       1 |    12
  0.08 s  [B] SQL> SELECT id_lote, stock FROM clinica.existencia WHERE id_lote = 10 AND id_sede = 1 FOR UPDATE NOWAIT;
  0.08 s  [B] --   NOWAIT: falla al instante en vez de esperar
  0.08 s  [B]      ERROR:  could not obtain lock on row in relation "existencia"
  0.08 s  [B] SQL> SET lock_timeout = '3s';
  0.08 s  [B] SQL> SELECT id_lote, stock FROM clinica.existencia WHERE id_lote = 10 AND id_sede = 1 FOR UPDATE;
  0.08 s  [B] --   con lock_timeout espera como máximo 3 segundos
  3.08 s  [B]      ERROR:  canceling statement due to lock timeout
  3.08 s  [B]      CONTEXT:  while locking tuple (0,88) in relation "existencia"
  3.08 s  [B] SQL> SELECT id_lote, stock FROM clinica.existencia WHERE id_lote = 10 AND id_sede = 1;
  3.08 s  [B] --   un SELECT normal (sin FOR UPDATE) NO espera
  3.08 s  [B]       id_lote | stock 
  3.08 s  [B]      ---------+-------
  3.08 s  [B]            10 |    12
  3.08 s  [B] SQL> RESET lock_timeout;
  3.08 s  [A] SQL> ROLLBACK;
